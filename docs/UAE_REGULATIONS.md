# Dubai / UAE Site Compliance Quick Reference

> Orientation only. Figures and procedures change. Always confirm against current authority circulars, the project specification and consultant instructions.

## Dubai Municipality (DM)
- Building control inspections are requested through DM's channels after consultant approval.
- Keep stamped, current approved drawings on site.
- Waste: use DM-approved carriers/disposal sites and keep transfer tickets.
- Dewatering discharge requires approval and water quality checks (turbidity, salinity, pH, hydrocarbons).
- Noise: noisy work near residential areas is restricted to permitted hours; check the current rule and any night-work permit.
- Hot-weather concreting: follow the project specification (commonly ~32 C maximum at placing).

## Trakhees (PCFC)
- Applies in Trakhees-regulated zones. Confirm required EHS permits and registrations for your works.
- Excavation, shoring and dewatering typically need monitoring plans and reporting.

## DEWA
- Obtain NOCs and service drawings before excavation. Hand-dig and detect near live services.
- Follow DEWA clearance and protection requirements for cables and pipes.

## Dubai Civil Defence (DCD)
- Fire protection and firestopping materials/systems must be DCD approved and installed per approved details.

## RTA
- Approvals and traffic management needed for works affecting public roads (pumps, deliveries, closures).

## MOHRE / Midday Work Ban
- Outdoor work in direct sun banned 12:30 PM - 3:00 PM, 15 June - 15 September.
- Provide shade, cool water and rest areas. Penalties apply; verify the current penalty rules.
