# Site Checklists

## Pre-Pour Structural Concrete
- [ ] WIR approved by consultant (formwork, rebar, MEP, embedded items)
- [ ] Cover spacers fitted and cover confirmed
- [ ] Tie-wire ends turned away from formwork faces
- [ ] Forms clean; cleanouts blown out and closed; no standing water
- [ ] Waterstops fixed and protected
- [ ] Mix design, supplier and hot-weather plan approved
- [ ] Concrete temperature limit and slump tolerance agreed
- [ ] Cube moulds, slump kit, thermometer, curing materials ready
- [ ] Pump, vibrators and standby equipment tested
- [ ] Pour sequence, truck intervals and crew assigned
- [ ] Permits in place (road/RTA, night work, lifting)
- [ ] Sunshades/windbreaks/fog misting ready if hot or windy

## Post-Pour
- [ ] Curing started immediately and logged
- [ ] Cubes made, labelled and stored/transferred correctly
- [ ] Delivery tickets reconciled with volume
- [ ] Temperatures and slump recorded per truck
- [ ] Defects photographed and reported

## Handover / Snagging
- [ ] Snag list by location with photo and responsible trade
- [ ] Tests and certificates collected (water-tightness, fire, MEP, pull-off)
- [ ] As-built markups complete
- [ ] O&M manuals and warranties delivered
- [ ] Consultant sign-offs recorded
